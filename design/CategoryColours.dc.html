Design system · Sicilian Barter
Main category colours
One colour per main category (27). Hues step by the golden angle (137.5°) at a fixed lightness and chroma, so categories that sit next to each other in an alphabetical list never look alike. The colour is an identity marker — a dot, an accent edge, a bar — never a background behind text.
